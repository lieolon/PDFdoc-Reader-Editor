PDFdoc is a completely free PDF app. Every feature others put behind a subscription is free here. No ads, subscriptions, accounts or analytics. The app has no internet access: your documents never leave your device.

<b>Viewing and reading</b>
• Smooth scrolling, zoom, night mode, page layouts and tabs
• Search, table of contents, bookmarks, clickable links
• Select and copy text, translate, share
• Read aloud, reading mode with large text, presentation, auto-scroll
• Two documents side by side

<b>Editing and comments</b>
• Highlighter, underline, pen, smart pen, eraser, shapes, text, notes, stamps
• Replace text, edit pictures of the document, add links
• Comments panel with replies
• Distance, perimeter and area measuring with a scale
• Create form fields and fill PDF forms

<b>Signatures and security</b>
• Handwritten signatures and certificate-based digital signatures, signature check
• Password (AES-256), print and copy restrictions
• True redaction: words, phone numbers, e-mails, card numbers removed for good
• Remove metadata and hidden information

<b>Scanner and text recognition</b>
• Auto-capture, edge detection, ID card and book modes, shadow removal
• Offline text recognition in 10 languages

<b>Tools</b>
• Pages: rotate, delete, reorder, extract, insert, crop margins, resize
• Merge, split, compress, watermarks, page numbers, headers and footers
• Table of contents, attachments, document properties, PDF/A for archiving
• Compare two PDFs
• Export to Word, Excel, EPUB, HTML, Markdown, TXT, JPG/PNG; PDF from photos and text
• Batch processing, folders, full-text search across documents, fingerprint lock

Open source. Only free libraries are used.
