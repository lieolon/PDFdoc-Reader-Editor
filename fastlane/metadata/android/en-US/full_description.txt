PDFdoc is a one-time purchase PDF app: no ads, subscriptions, accounts or analytics. Every feature is available right away. The app has no internet access: your documents never leave your device.

<b>Viewing</b>
• Smooth scrolling, pinch to zoom, night mode
• Modes: continuous, one page at a time, two-page spread
• Text search with highlights, table of contents and bookmarks
• Password-protected documents, printing, sharing

<b>Editing</b>
• Highlight, underline, strike out, pen, shapes, arrows
• Text (including Cyrillic), notes, images, stamps
• Replace text, undo and redo
• Edits are saved as standard annotations that other apps can show

<b>Signatures and forms</b>
• Handwritten signature drawn with a finger or imported from a photo, stored encrypted
• Fill in PDF forms

<b>Scanner and OCR</b>
• Camera scanning with automatic edge detection and perspective correction
• Filters: enhanced, black & white, grayscale
• Offline text recognition for Russian and English

<b>Tools</b>
• Pages: rotate, delete, reorder, extract, insert, duplicate
• Merge and split PDFs, compress, watermarks, page numbers, headers and footers
• Password and restrictions (AES-256), password removal
• Export to TXT, DOCX (text only), JPG/PNG; create PDF from photos

Open source. Only free libraries are used: PdfBox-Android, Tesseract, CameraX, Jetpack.
